\subsection{Cartan Structure Equations for Lie Group}

The Maurer-Cartan equation is intrinsic to the group; it's independent of the connection.
The Cartan structure equations,
in the other hand, assume a connection.
To define 
define $\pform{\omega}{}$, you must choose a connection—most commonly the Levi-Civita connection of a chosen left-invariant Riemannian metric.

Let $G$ be an
$n$ dim Lie group
with $\ger{g}=
T_e(G)$.
Choose a basis $\vecE_\mu$ (these are  left invariant vector fields that map $g\in G$ to
$\vecE_\mu(g)$) of $\ger{g}$
such that

\beq
[\vecE_\mu, \vecE_\nu]=
C\indices{^\rho_{\mu\nu}}
\vecE_\rho
\eeq
Expand 

\beq
\pform{\vTheta}{}=
\pform{\Theta^\alp}{}\vecE_\alp
\eeq
Assume

\beq
\ul{\Theta^\alp}(\vecE_\beta)=
\delta^\alp_\beta
\eeq

Recall we 
proved

\beq
\ul{d\Theta^\alp}=-
C\indices{^\alp_{\mu\nu}}
\ul{\Theta^\mu}
\wedge
\ul{\Theta^\nu}
\eeq
